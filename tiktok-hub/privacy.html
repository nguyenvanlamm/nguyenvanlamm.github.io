Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23 · Operator: Nguyen Van Lam · Contact: lamnv260398@gmail.com

1. What TikTok Hub is

TikTok Hub ("the app") is a self-hosted tool. It runs on a computer or server controlled by its operator and is used to manage the operator's own TikTok accounts through TikTok's official APIs. There is no central TikTok Hub server.

2. Data we access from TikTok

Only after you authorize the app through TikTok's consent screen, and only for the permissions (scopes) you grant:

  • Profile (user.info.basic, user.info.profile): open ID, display name, username, avatar, bio, profile link, verified status.
  • Statistics (user.info.stats): follower, following, likes and video counts.
  • Public videos (video.list): video IDs, captions, cover images, links, and view/like/comment/share counts.
  • Posting (video.publish, video.upload): used only to upload videos and captions that you choose, and to read their publishing status.
  • Comments (TikTok API for Business: comment.list, comment.list.manage — only for accounts you connect to it): comments and replies on your own videos, including the commenter's username, display name and avatar, so you can read them in one inbox and reply, like, hide or delete your own replies.
  • Insights (TikTok API for Business: user.insights, video.insights — only for accounts you connect to it): aggregated analytics of your own account and videos, such as views, reach, watch time, traffic sources and follower demographics (age range, gender and country shares, never individual followers).

The page biz-callback/ on this website only forwards the TikTok authorization code to the app running on your own computer. It stores nothing and sends the code nowhere else.

3. How the data is used

To show your accounts and statistics in the app, to publish or schedule the content you prepare, and to keep growth history. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Where the data is stored

In a local database on the operator's machine. OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest. Videos you select are copied to the app's local data folder. Data is sent only to TikTok, through its official APIs, when you perform an action.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the operator deletes it. Disconnecting an account in the app revokes the authorization at TikTok and deletes its tokens. You can also remove access at any time in the TikTok app: Profile → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
